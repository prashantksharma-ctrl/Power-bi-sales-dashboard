# Power-bi-sales-dashboard
Interactive Sales Dashboard built using Power BI, DAX and Power Query to analyse sales, profit, orders and business performance.
# Sales Overview Dashboard 
# Key KPI 
1 Total Sales
2 Total Profit
3 Total Order
4 PROFIT MARGIN 
5 AVG ORDER VALUE
# Dashboard Features
1 Monthly and Yearly Order by Trends
2 Sales and Profit by Month
3 Sales by Category
4 Profit by State
5 Total sales by Category

# Product & Customer Dashboard
# Key KPI
1 Total Product Category
2 Total Products Sales
3 Total Customers
4 Customer Lifetime Value
5 AVG ORDER VALUE
# Dashboard Features
1 Product-wise Profit vs Sales 
2 (Products) Sub-Category by Sales
3 Customer Orders Month/Year Trends 
4 Profit Share by Category Sub-Category
5 Top 10 Products by Sales
